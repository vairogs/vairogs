# vairogs

> ⚠️ **Deprecated — this ecosystem has moved to [@valksor](https://github.com/valksor).**
>
> The Vairogs PHP/Symfony libraries are continued under the **valksor** organisation as the `php-*` packages.
> The `vairogs/vairogs` meta-package is succeeded by **[valksor/php-valksor](https://github.com/valksor/php-valksor)**, and each sub-library maps to `valksor/php-<name>`:
>
> | vairogs (old) | valksor (current) |
> | --- | --- |
> | `vairogs/vairogs` | [`valksor/php-valksor`](https://github.com/valksor/php-valksor) |
> | `vairogs/functions` | [`valksor/php-functions`](https://github.com/valksor/php-functions) |
> | `vairogs/functions-web` | [`valksor/php-functions-web`](https://github.com/valksor/php-functions-web) |
> | `vairogs/bundle` | [`valksor/php-bundle`](https://github.com/valksor/php-bundle) |
> | `vairogs/doctrine-tools` | [`valksor/php-doctrine-tools`](https://github.com/valksor/php-doctrine-tools) |
>
> Please update your dependencies. New work happens at **[github.com/valksor](https://github.com/valksor)**.
